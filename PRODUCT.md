## Platform
web

## Users
Mid-market operators and enterprise teams evaluating applied AI for operations, customer experience, finance, and data workflows. This audience is explicit in the user brief and existing site.

## Product Purpose
FraterAI pairs strategy, data engineering, AI development, deployment, and team enablement to bring AI into real operational workflows.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing Next.js marketing routes, service and solution slugs, contact field names/order, lead intake API, scheduling, authentication, and portal behavior must remain functional. The redesign covers the public marketing website. The portal is outside the visual redesign.

## Brand Commitments
The user supplied five visual references: paper surfaces, blue engraved illustrations, editorial serif headings, restrained classical architecture. Buildings should suggest academic campuses inspired by Low Memorial Library, Penn Museum, Stanford Main Quad, Doheny Library, and Bovard, without copying governmental buildings. Preserve FraterAI identity.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing route content and the user's visual mockups. Mockup portraits, customer quotations, dashboard figures, and performance statistics are treated as illustrative pending verification. Do not invent customers or publish mock figures as measured results.
